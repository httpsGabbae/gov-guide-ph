# Philippine Health Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth) — Citizen's Charter

## Agency Overview
- **Agency:** Philippine Health Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth)
- **Parent Department:** Department of Health (DOH)
- **Service Name:** Member Registration & Issuance of PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN) / Member Data Record (MDR)
- **Legal Mandate:** Republic Act No. 7875 (National Health Insurance Act of 1995) as amended by Republic Act No. 11223 (Universal Health Care Act)
- **Official Online Member Portal:** https://memberinquiry.philhealth.gov.ph
- **Corporate Action Center Hotline:** (02) 8441-7442 / Callback SMS: 0921-630-0009
- **Official Inquiries Email:** actioncenter@philhealth.gov.ph
- **Head Office:** Citystate Centre, 709 Shaw Boulevard, Pasig City, Metro Manila

## 1. Membership Categories

### A. Direct Contributors
Individuals who have the capacity to pay premiums, including:
1. Formally Employed (Government and Private sector employees)
2. Self-Earning Individuals, Freelancers, and Professionals
3. Overseas Filipino Workers (OFWs)
4. Kasambahays (Domestic Workers under RA 10361)

### B. Indirect Contributors
Individuals whose premiums are subsidized by the national government, including:
1. Indigents certified by the Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD) via Listahanan
2. Beneficiaries of Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program (4Ps)
3. Senior Citizens (Republic Act No. 10645)
4. Persons with Disability (PWDs under Republic Act No. 11228)

## 2. Documentary Requirements

### A. Standard Requirements for Direct Contributors
1. **PhilHealth Member Registration Form (PMRF):** Two (2) duly accomplished original copies.
2. **Valid Identification:** One (1) primary government ID or two (2) secondary IDs:
   - Primary: PhilSys National ID, DFA Passport, Driver's License, SSS/GSIS UMID, PRC ID.
   - Secondary: Postal ID, Voter's Certificate, Company ID, Barangay ID.
3. **ID Photos:** Two (2) pieces 1x1 recent colored ID photos with white background.
4. **Proof of Income (For Self-Employed / Freelancers):** Latest Income Tax Return (ITR), financial statement, or notarized declaration of monthly income.

### B. Requirements for Declaring Qualified Dependents
1. **Legal Spouse (Non-member or non-working):** Photocopy of PSA Marriage Certificate.
2. **Biological Children (Below 21 years old, unmarried, not employed):** Photocopy of PSA Birth Certificate.
3. **Children with Mental or Physical Disability (21 years old and above):** Medical Certificate issued by attending licensed physician.
4. **Parents (60 years old and above, non-members):** Photocopy of PSA Birth Certificate of the member and Parent's Senior Citizen ID or birth certificate.

### C. Requirements for Senior Citizens (Under RA 10645)
1. Duly accomplished PMRF.
2. One (1) 1x1 photo.
3. Senior Citizen ID issued by OSCA (Office of Senior Citizens Affairs) or PSA Birth Certificate proving age 60+.

## 3. Step-by-Step Procedure (Local Health Insurance Office / PhilHealth Express)

| Step | Citizen Action | Agency Action | Counter / Window | Processing Time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Step 1: Submission & Verification** | Submit completed PMRF and supporting valid IDs and dependent birth certificates. | Reviews form completeness, cross-checks dependent documents. | Frontline Evaluation Counter | 5-10 minutes |
| **Step 2: Member Encoding & Fingerprint** | Present biometrics or confirm identity details at the encoder window. | Checks database for existing records (duplicate check); generates unique 12-digit PIN. | Encoding Station | 10 minutes |
| **Step 3: MDR & ID Card Printing** | Review personal details on the printed Member Data Record (MDR). | Prints official Member Data Record (MDR) and PhilHealth Identification Card (PVC/Paper). | Releasing Window | 5 minutes |
| **Step 4: Initial Premium Contribution** | (Direct contributors only) Pay initial premium at cashier or accredited bank/GCash. | Issues Official Receipt (OR) or validates digital payment receipt. | Cashier Counter | 5 minutes |

## 4. Fees & Premium Contribution Rates

- **Registration and PIN Card Issuance Fee:** **₱0.00 (FREE)**
- **MDR (Member Data Record) Printout:** **₱0.00 (FREE)**
- **Monthly Contribution Rates (Universal Health Care Act):**
  - **Premium Rate:** 5% of monthly basic salary (shared 50-50 between employer and employee for formal sector; 100% for self-employed).
  - **Income Floor:** ₱10,000.00 (Minimum monthly premium: ₱500.00)
  - **Income Ceiling:** ₱100,000.00 (Maximum monthly premium: ₱5,000.00)
  - **Senior Citizens & Indigents:** **₱0.00 contribution paid by citizen** (fully subsidized by National Government).

## 5. Critical Inspection Checkpoints & Red Flags

- **Duplicate PIN Prevention:** Citizens must NEVER register for a second PIN. If you forgot your PhilHealth number, request a "Verification of Existing PIN" rather than filing a new PMRF.
- **Dependent Age Limit:** Dependent children automatically age out on their 21st birthday, unless physically or mentally incapacitated and permanently dependent.
- **Name Spelling:** In case of discrepancies between PSA Birth Certificate and ID, the PSA SECPA birth certificate is the controlling document.
