# Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) — Citizen's Charter

## Agency Overview
- **Agency:** Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) / Pangasiwaan ng Estadistika ng Pilipinas
- **Parent Department:** National Economic and Development Authority (NEDA)
- **Service Name:** Issuance of Certified Copy of Certificate of Live Birth in Security Paper (SECPA)
- **Legal Mandate:** Republic Act No. 10625 (Philippine Statistical Act of 2013) and Act No. 3753 (Civil Registry Law)
- **Official Online Portals:** https://psaserbilis.com.ph / https://psahelpline.ph
- **Public Assistance Hotline:** (02) 8461-0500 / 0917-872-7722
- **Official Inquiries Email:** info@psa.gov.ph / civilregistry@psa.gov.ph
- **Head Office:** PSA Complex, East Avenue, Diliman, Quezon City 1101

---

## 1. Service Description & Eligibility

### Service Scope
Issuance of an official copy of a Certificate of Live Birth printed on security paper (SECPA) containing specialized microprint, barcode, and watermark to prevent fraudulent tampering.

### Authorized Requesters (Strict Confidentiality under RA 10173 & PD 603)
Under Section 7 of Presidential Decree No. 603, birth records are strictly confidential and may ONLY be released to:
1. The **Document Owner** (at least 18 years of age)
2. The **Parent** (Mother or Father acknowledged on the birth certificate)
3. The **Spouse** (Must present PSA Marriage Certificate)
4. The **Direct Descendant** (Child of legal age; must present proof of relationship)
5. The **Legal Guardian** or Court-Authorized Representative (Must present Court Order or Duly Notarized Special Power of Attorney).

## 2. Documentary Requirements

### A. If the Requester is the Document Owner or Parent
1. Duly accomplished **PSA Birth Certificate Application Form (Yellow Form)**.
2. Original and clear photocopy of **One (1) Valid Government-Issued Primary ID**:
   - PhilSys National ID (Physical or ePhilID)
   - DFA Passport
   - LTO Driver's License
   - PRC ID / UMID / SSS ID
   - Digitized Postal ID

### B. If the Requester is an Authorized Representative (Non-Immediate Family)
1. **Special Power of Attorney (SPA)** or signed **Authorization Letter** explicitly stating the specific civil registry document and purpose.
2. Original and photocopy of the **Document Owner's Valid ID**.
3. Original and photocopy of the **Authorized Representative's Valid ID**.

### C. If the Document Owner is a Minor (Below 18 years old)
- Must be requested directly by the mother or father.
- If requested by guardian, must present proof of guardianship (Court Guardianship Order or DSWD Certificate).

## 3. Step-by-Step Procedure (Walk-in via PSA CRS Outlet)

| Step | Citizen Action | Agency Action | Counter / Window | Processing Time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Step 1: Appointment & Queue** | Book online CRS appointment at `appointment.psa.gov.ph`. Present QR code at entrance. | Scans appointment QR code and issues Queue Ticket. | Security & Helpdesk | 5 minutes |
| **Step 2: Form Evaluation** | Submit filled application form, valid IDs, and supporting authorization at the screening window. | Evaluates requester eligibility and verifies ID authenticity. | Screening Counter | 5-10 minutes |
| **Step 3: Cashier Payment** | Pay the statutory civil registry document fee in exact cash or digital wallet. | Collects payment, enters transaction into CRS, and prints Official Receipt (OR). | Cashier Counter | 5 minutes |
| **Step 4: Database Search & Encoding** | Wait for electronic verification against the Civil Registry Database. | Encodes registry number, performs visual index search, and prints document on SECPA. | CRS Processing Back-End | 30-45 minutes |
| **Step 5: Document Releasing** | Present OR and claim stub at the releasing counter. Inspect printed details. | Validates claim stub, inspects dry seal, releases birth certificate. | Releasing Station | 5-10 minutes |

## 4. Processing Fees & Turnaround Time

### Walk-in at PSA CRS Outlets
- **Fee per Copy:** **₱155.00** (inclusive of documentary stamp tax).
- **Processing Time:** **Same-day release** (usually 1 to 2 hours upon payment).

### Online Door-to-Door Delivery (PSA Serbilis / Helpline)
- **Fee per Copy:** **₱330.00** to **₱365.00** (inclusive of delivery and courier handling).
- **Metro Manila Delivery:** 3 to 5 working days.
- **Provincial Delivery:** 6 to 8 working days.

## 5. Critical Inspection Checkpoints (Avoiding Counter Rejection)

- **Legibility Check:** If the local civil registry copy on file is blurred, unreadable, or torn, the applicant must provide a clear certified transcription (Form 1A) from the Local Civil Registry Office (LCRO) where the birth was recorded.
- **Name Inconsistencies:** If there is a typographical error in the first name or date of birth, an administrative correction under RA 9048 or RA 10172 must be filed with the LCRO, not via standard request.
- **SECPA Barcode:** Do not fold, wet, or laminate the SECPA birth certificate; laminating damages security thermal threads.
