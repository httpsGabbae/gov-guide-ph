---

## 1. What is PSAHelpline.ph & Main Use Cases
**PSAHelpline.ph** is an authorized, government-partnered platform that allows Filipinos in the Philippines to order official civil registry documents online without visiting or lining up at a PSA Civil Registry System (CRS) outlet.

### Main Use Cases:
1. **Zero "Balik-Pila" Convenience:** Citizens do not need to wake up at 4:00 AM, book weeks in advance on the PSA CRS appointment portal, or take time off work. Documents are delivered directly to home or workplace addresses nationwide.
2. **First-Time Jobseekers & Students:** Avoid costly travel expenses to regional centers; order NBI, DFA, or PhilHealth requirements from home.
3. **Overseas & Provincial Families:** Relatives in provinces can order documents delivered directly to their doorstep without travelling to provincial capitals.
4. **Instant Digital Copies:** PSA E-Certificates provide instant, tamper-evident digital verification for digital job applications.

## 3. Authorized Recipients & Privacy Rules (RA 10173 & PD 603)

Under Section 7 of Presidential Decree No. 603 and RA 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012), PSA civil registry documents cannot be handed over to unauthorized third parties.

### Allowed Requesters & Receivers:
1. **The Document Owner** (if of legal age, 18+).
2. **The Parent** (named in the document).
3. **The Spouse** (must present valid ID and proof of marriage if receiving on behalf).
4. **The Direct Child** (must be 18+; presents proof of filiation).
5. **Authorized Representative:** If the owner cannot receive the package personally upon courier delivery:
   - Must complete the online **Letter of Authorization (LOA)** on PSAHelpline.ph.
   - The representative must present:
     1. Signed LOA from the document owner.
     2. Original valid government-issued photo ID of the representative.
     3. Original or clear photocopy of the valid ID of the document owner.

---

## 4. Documentary Requirements for Receiving Delivery

When the courier arrives, the receiver MUST present:
- **For Document Owner:** Exactly **One (1) Original Valid Government-Issued Photo ID**:
  - PhilSys National ID (Physical or ePhilID)
  - DFA Passport
  - Driver's License
  - UMID / SSS ID / GSIS eCard
  - PRC License
  - Digitized Postal ID
  - Voter's ID / Senior Citizen ID
- **For Representative:** Original LOA + Representative's Valid ID + Owner's Valid ID copy.
