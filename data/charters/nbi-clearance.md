# National Bureau of Investigation (NBI) — Citizen's Charter

## Agency Overview
- **Agency:** National Bureau of Investigation (NBI) / Pambansang Kawanihan ng Pagsisiyasat
- **Parent Department:** Department of Justice (DOJ)
- **Service Name:** Issuance of NBI Clearance (First-Time Jobseeker & Renewal/Regular)
- **Legal Mandate:** Republic Act No. 10867 (NBI Reorganization and Modernization Act) and Republic Act No. 11261 (First-Time Jobseekers Assistance Act)
- **Official Portal:** https://clearance.nbi.gov.ph
- **Public Assistance Hotline:** (02) 8523-8231 to 38 / 0961-734-7222
- **Official Support Email:** clearance@nbi.gov.ph
- **Head Office:** NBI Clearance Building, UN Avenue, Ermita, Manila, 1000

---

## 1. Classification & Beneficiaries

### Regular Applicants
Filipino citizens needing clearance for local employment, travel abroad, promotion, visa application, adoption, or business permit.

### First-Time Jobseekers (RA 11261)
Beneficiaries are entitled to one (1) free NBI clearance certificate upon submission of an official Barangay Certificate of First-Time Jobseeker and a signed Oath of Undertaking.

## 2. Documentary Requirements

### A. Primary Identification (Present at least TWO valid government-issued IDs)
1. **PhilSys National ID** (Physical card or ePhilID printed on paper with valid QR)
2. **Philippine Passport** (Valid for at least 6 months)
3. **Driver's License** (LTO plastic card or official electronic license)
4. **UMID / SSS ID**
5. **PRC Professional Identification Card**
6. **Postal ID** (Digitized PVC card)
7. **Voter's ID or COMELEC Certificate of Registration**
<!-- TODO: verify if PhilHealth laminated ID card is still accepted as secondary ID in 2026 -->

### B. Special Requirements for First-Time Jobseekers
1. **Barangay Certification:** Original copy issued by the Punong Barangay stating that the applicant is a first-time jobseeker under RA 11261. Must be issued within ninety (90) days.
2. **Oath of Undertaking:** Duly accomplished and signed oath form issued by the barangay.
3. **One (1) photocopy of Barangay Certification.**

### C. Requirements for Renewal with Previous NBI Clearance
1. **Old NBI Clearance Certificate** (Issued 2014 onwards) or NBI ID number.
2. If personal details changed (e.g., married name for female applicants): Original PSA Marriage Certificate on SECPA paper.

## 3. Step-by-Step Procedure

| Step | Citizen Action | Agency Action | Office / Counter | Turnaround |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Step 1: Online Registration** | Register or log in at `clearance.nbi.gov.ph`, fill out personal data sheet, and book an appointment slot. | System generates Reference Number. | Online | 10 minutes |
| **Step 2: Fee Payment** | Pay fee via online channels (GCash, Maya, 7-Eleven, Bank) using Reference Number. *First-time jobseekers skip payment by presenting RA 11261 Barangay Cert at counter.* | Validates reference number and issues official electronic receipt. | E-Payment / Landbank | Instant |
| **Step 3: Biometrics & Photo Capture** | Proceed to chosen NBI branch on appointment date. Present 2 valid IDs and Reference Number. | Encodes photo, fingerprints, and digital signature. Verifies identity. | NBI Processing Counter | 10-15 minutes |
| **Step 4: Clearance Evaluation (HIT Check)** | Wait for automated system check against derogatory records. | Database cross-match against warrants and criminal database. | Evaluation Unit | Instant (No HIT) / 5-10 Days (With HIT) |
| **Step 5: Release of Clearance** | Claim printed NBI clearance certificate with security watermark. | Prints and dry-seals NBI Clearance. | Printing & Releasing Station | 5 minutes |

## 5. Validity & "HIT" Procedures

- **Validity:** The NBI Clearance Certificate is valid for **one (1) year** from the date of issuance.
- **Handling a "HIT":**
  - A "HIT" occurs when an applicant's name matches or resembles a person with pending criminal warrants or court cases.
  - The applicant is advised to return for a "Quality Interview" after **five (5) to ten (10) working days**.
  - If the case is dismissed, bring a certified true copy of the Court Dismissal or Certificate of Finality from the trial court.
